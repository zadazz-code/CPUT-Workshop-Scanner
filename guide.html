1. Sign in

You need your staff PIN and the Workshop Tools app. Open it from the home-screen icon, or from the link you were given.

  1. Tap Staff sign in at the top right. The cursor is already in the PIN box.
  2. Type your PIN and press Enter, or tap Sign in.
  3. Your name appears at the top. The app signs you out after 10 minutes without use, so sign out when you leave by tapping Sign out.

To return to the student screen, tap Student sign in at the top right.

After you sign in, the screen is empty. Nothing from an earlier session is shown, so a tool cannot be checked in by mistake.

2. Types of item

There are three kinds of item. The app treats each kind differently.

KindExamplesHow it is recordedComes back?
ToolHammers, saws, grindersScan its own QR label, such as TL-042.Yes. Check it in when it is returned.
AccessoryDrill bits, allen keys, hole sawsScan the family label (for example DB), then pick the item. One item at a time.Yes. Tick each item when it is returned.
ConsumableSanding paper, welding rodsScan the family label (for example SP), then pick the item and how many.No. These are used up.
Tools in repair. A tool with the status Repair cannot be checked out. Scanning it or typing its number shows currently unavailable due to repairs. Nothing is added to the list.

3. The screen and the bar

The screen runs top to bottom in this order: the Student box, then Scan tools (the camera), then the Out to this student bar with the count, then To check out and To check in.

Tap the Out to this student bar to open the full list, and tap it again to close it. The list closes by itself after each save. It stays open while you have ticked items, so a tick cannot be hidden by accident.

A bar runs along the bottom of the screen while you are signed in. It shows how many items are waiting and holds the one main button:

  • If items are waiting to be checked out, the button says Check out with the number. A student must be confirmed first.
  • If you have ticked items from a student's list, the button says Check in with that number.
  • If only items to check in are waiting, the button says Check in with that number.
Saving is done from the bar only. Each save asks you to Confirm first. If the button is grey, there is nothing to save yet.

4. Use the 2D scanner

The handheld scanner reads the QR labels on tools and the family and student codes. It works like a keyboard. Each scan types the code into the box that has the cursor, then presses Enter for you.

The cursor decides where a scan goes. Before you scan, check that the cursor is in the right box:
  • Student number box: when the app opens, and after you sign in. Scan the student card.
  • Tool box (Or type a number or code): after a student is confirmed, and after each save. Scan tools here.
If the cursor has moved, click into the box you want, then scan.

Scanning a session, step by step

  1. Scan the student card. The number goes in and is confirmed straight away. The cursor moves to the tool box.
  2. Scan each tool. Its name appears at once with Checking…, then the result. Keep scanning. Each scan adds one tool to the list.
  3. Scan a family code, such as DB, to open the picker for accessories or consumables. See Take accessories.
  4. Press Enter on an empty tool box to open the check-out summary. Choose Confirm or Cancel with the arrow keys, then press Enter. See Check out a tool.

Scanner setup

The lecturer sets the scanner up once, using the scanner's manual. It must be set to:

  • USB keyboard mode, so it types into any box on the screen.
  • Enter after each scan, so every scan is confirmed without pressing a key.
  • American keyboard layout and capital letters, so codes such as WR-2.5 are typed correctly.
  • Trigger mode: press the trigger for each scan.

Labels are QR codes at the moment. Code 128 barcodes may be added later. The scanner reads both.

5. Check out a tool

  1. Confirm the student. Scan the student card or tap Scan student QR. You can also type the student number. Press Enter, or tap Confirm student.
  2. Scan each tool. Point the scanner or the camera at the tool's QR label. It appears under To check out. If a label is damaged, type its number (for example 42 for TL-042) and press Enter.
  3. Check the return date. It starts as today. Change it only if the tools are due back on another day.
  4. Check out. Press Enter on the empty tool box, or tap Check out in the bar. The summary opens.
  5. Confirm or cancel the summary (see the box below), then press Enter.

6. Take accessories

Accessories have a family label, for example DB for drill bits. Scanning it opens the list of items in that family. Items are shown by name, so there is no code to read.

  1. Confirm the student first.
  2. Scan the family label, for example DB. The picker opens with the first item in stock already selected. You can also type DB in the tool box and press Enter.
  3. Choose the item. Press ↓ or ↑ to move through the items, then press Enter. An accessory is added to the list at once, and the picker stays open.
  4. Take another by moving to the next item and pressing Enter again, or scan another label.
  5. Finish the picker. Press ↓ until Add to check out is highlighted, then press Enter. The picker closes and the cursor returns to the tool box.
  6. Check out. Press Enter on the empty tool box. Choose Confirm on the summary, then press Enter.

7. Take consumables

Consumables are used up, so there is nothing to bring back.

  1. Confirm the student, then scan the family label, for example SP. Choose the item with ↓ or ↑, then press Enter.
  2. Set how many. The quantity box starts at 1. Type a number, or tap + or −. The total price updates as you do. Press Enter to log it.
  3. Add more or finish. Repeat for other items, or move to Add to check out and press Enter.
  4. Check out. Press Enter on the empty tool box. Read the summary, tell the student the Total to pay, choose Confirm, and press Enter.
Prices. Each block shows the price of one item. The summary before you confirm shows the price and total of each line. After saving, the message shows what was taken and its total.

8. Check in a tool

There are two ways to check a tool in. Use whichever is quicker.

Option A: scan the tool

  1. Scan the tool's QR label, or type its number in the tool box.
  2. It appears under To check in. Press Enter on the empty tool box, or tap Check in in the bar. Read the summary, choose Confirm, and press Enter.

Option B: from the student's list

  1. Confirm the student. The Out to this student bar shows how many items they have. Tap it to open the list.
  2. Tap each tool that has come back. Its row turns green. Tap it again to unselect it.
  3. Tap Check in in the bar at the bottom, read the summary, and tap Confirm.

9. Return accessories

  1. Confirm the student. Each accessory they have out has its own row.
  2. Tick one row for each item that is coming back.
  3. Check in. Tap Check in in the bar at the bottom, read the summary, and tap Confirm.

10. Who has this tool?

This only looks up a tool. It does not change anything.

  1. Tap Who has this? above the scan box.
  2. Scan the tool's label, or type its number.
  3. A card shows who the tool is out to, their year, and the return date. An available tool says Not out to anyone.
  4. Tap Add to lists to go back to checking items out and in.

11. Undo a mistake

  1. After a save, a message appears at the bottom with an Undo button. It stays for about 10 seconds.
  2. Tap Undo to reverse the save. Tools checked out are checked back in. Tools checked in are signed out to the same student again.
What undo cannot do. It works for tools only, and only for the most recent save. Refreshing the page or signing out clears it. Accessories and consumables cannot be undone from the app. If one was taken by mistake, tell the lecturer so the stock can be corrected from the Log.

12. Scan feedback and colours

Every scan gives a clear response, so you don't have to check the list to know it worked.

The item's row also flashes green in the list. Android phones beep and vibrate. iPhones show the flash only.

Colour key

Green flash: the scan worked.
Red flash: the label is not a storeroom item, cannot be found, or the tool is in repair.
Light red row: due back today.
Dark red row: overdue. Shown first in the student's list, with a banner.
Green row: selected for check-in.
Light red block (picker): low stock. Tell the lecturer so it can be reordered.
Dark red block (picker): no stock. It cannot be picked.

13. Students

Students do not need a PIN. On the first screen they:

  1. Scan their student card, or type their student number.
  2. Their tools, accessories and return dates appear straight away. Overdue tools are dark red and listed first.

Students see only their own items. Accessories show the date they were taken. They have no return date, so students return them to the storeroom when they have finished with them.

14. If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
A scan goes into the wrong box, or nothing happensClick into the box you want (the student box or the tool box), then scan again.
The scanner types letters or numbers wronglyCheck the scanner is set to American keyboard layout and capitals, as in section 4.
The arrow keys do not move the highlightClick once on the screen to make sure the page has focus, then try again. Hard-refresh the page if it still does nothing.
The camera won't startAllow camera access when the phone asks. Check that no other app is using the camera.
The image is too small or blurryOn Android, drag the Zoom in slider. On iPhone, move the phone closer to the label, about 15 to 20 cm away.
"That QR code is not a storeroom tool"The label may be damaged or from another item. Type the number instead.
"Tool is already out"Someone has already checked it out. Use Who has this? to find out who.
"is currently unavailable due to repairs"The tool is in repair and cannot be checked out. Tell the lecturer if it is needed.
"Only 3 of Drill bit 3 mm in stock"There are not enough on the shelf. Choose a smaller number, or check the shelf and tell the lecturer if the count looks wrong.
"Confirm a student first"Accessories and consumables are recorded against a student. Confirm the student, then scan the label again.
The family label does nothingCheck that it is the family code, for example DB. If it still does nothing, type the code in the tool box instead.
"Session expired. Sign in again."You were inactive for 10 minutes. Sign in again. Check your list, because it may be empty.
"Could not reach the storeroom server"There is no signal or Wi-Fi. Wait a moment and try again. Nothing is saved until the server answers.
"Student number not found"Check the number on the student card. Ask the lecturer to add the student if they are missing.
Keep your PIN private. Do not share it, and sign out when you leave the storeroom.
