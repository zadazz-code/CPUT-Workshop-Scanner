Workshop Tools Guide

How to check tools out and in, and how to find out who has a tool. For workshop staff.

What you need

Your phone, your staff PIN, and the Workshop Tools app. Open it from the home-screen icon, or from the link you were given.

Staff sign in

  1. Tap Staff sign in at the top right.
  2. Type your PIN and tap Sign in.
  3. Your name appears at the top. The app signs you out after 10 minutes without touching the screen, so sign out when you leave.

To go back to the student screen, tap Student sign in at the top right.

Check a tool out to a student

  1. Student. Tap Scan student QR and point the camera at the student card. Or type the student number. The student's name appears. Tap Confirm student if you typed it.
  2. Tool. Point the camera at the tool's QR label. The tool appears under To check out. If the label is damaged, tap the box, type the number (for example 42 for TL-042) and tap Add tool.
  3. Repeat step 2 for each tool. Each one is added to the list.
  4. Return date. The date is today. Change it only if the tools are due back on another day.
  5. Tap Check out. Read the summary and tap Confirm.
The list clears straight away. A short message appears at the bottom with an Undo button. See Undo a mistake below.

The bar at the bottom

While you are signed in, a bar runs along the bottom of the screen. It shows how many tools are waiting to be checked out or in, and it has one main button:

  • If tools are waiting to be checked out, the button says Check out with the number. Tap it to check them out. The student must be confirmed first.
  • If you have ticked tools from a student's list, the button says Check in with the number ticked.
  • If only tools to check in are waiting, the button says Check in with that number.

The bar does the same job as the buttons in each section, and it still asks you to Confirm first. If the button is grey, there is nothing to save yet.

Check a tool back in

Option A: scan the tool

  1. Scan the tool's QR label, or type its number.
  2. The tool appears under To check in. Tap Check in, read the summary, and tap Confirm.

Option B: from the student's list

  1. Confirm the student. Their tools appear under Out to this student.
  2. Tap each tool that has come back. Its row turns green. Tap again to unselect it.
  3. Tap Check in selected, read the summary, and tap Confirm.

Find out who has a tool

  1. Tap Who has this? above the scan box.
  2. Scan the tool's label, or type its number.
  3. A card shows who the tool is out to, the year, and the return date. An available tool says Not out to anyone.
  4. Tap Add to lists to go back to checking tools out and in.

Looking up a tool does not change anything.

Scan feedback

Every scan gives a clear response, so you don't have to look at the list to know it worked.

The tool's row also flashes green in the list. A beep and vibration sound on Android phones. iPhones show the flash only.

What the colours mean

Green flash: the scan worked.
Red flash: the label isn't a storeroom tool, or the tool can't be found.
Light red row: due back today.
Dark red row: overdue. Shown first in the student's list, with a banner.
Green row: selected for check-in.

Undo a mistake

  1. After a save, a message appears at the bottom with an Undo button. It stays for about 10 seconds.
  2. Tap Undo to reverse the save. Tools checked out come back in. Tools checked in are signed out to the same student again.

Only the most recent save can be undone. If you refresh the page or sign out, the undo is lost, so check the Log sheet instead.

Students: see your own tools

Students don't need a PIN. On the first screen they:

  1. Scan their student card, or type their student number.
  2. Their workshop tools and return dates appear straight away. Overdue tools are dark red and listed first.

Students see only their own tools and return dates. Staff don't need to do anything for this.

If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
The camera won't startAllow camera access when the phone asks. Check that no other app is using the camera.
The image is too small or blurryOn Android, drag the Zoom in slider. On iPhone, move the phone closer to the label, about 15 to 20 cm away.
"That QR code is not a storeroom tool"The label may be damaged or from another item. Type the number instead.
"Tool is already out"Someone has already checked it out. Use Who has this? to find out who.
"Session expired. Sign in again."You were inactive for 10 minutes. Sign in again. Your list may be empty, so check what you had before.
"Could not reach the storeroom server"There is no signal or Wi-Fi. Wait a moment and try again. Nothing is saved until the server answers.
"Student number not found"Check the number on the student card. Ask the lecturer to add the student if they are missing.
Keep your PIN private. Don't share it, and sign out when you leave the storeroom.
